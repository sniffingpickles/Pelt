# Pelt 1.1.0

- Spots skinnable corpses by the cursor too: if a corpse shows the skinning knife, Pelt knows, even without the tooltip line.
- Point at a waiting corpse and the card lights up with "Right-click to skin". Walking up to it while pointing at it no longer counts as walking away.
- The card fills up while you skin, and goes back if the cast is interrupted.
- Skill-ups show on the "Skinned" card, and `/pelt stats` counts them.
- Sound pickers are now dropdowns, with a play button next to each one and on every sound in the list.
- More sounds: six made for Pelt, 28 from the game, plus EllesmereUI's sounds and any SharedMedia sound packs you have.
- Other people's kills remind you too when you point at or target them, as "Up for grabs". Anyone can skin a looted corpse. Passing one up doesn't count as a miss, and a new setting turns them off.
- `/pelt check` shows what Pelt can read off your target, to help when a corpse isn't picked up.

# Pelt 1.0.0

First release.

- A reminder card for corpses you can skin, with the skill each one needs and yours.
- Catches corpses that dropped no loot, once the fight ends.
- Shakes, plays a sound and shows big text when you walk away without skinning.
- Ticks the corpse off when you skin it, with what you got and your streak.
- Counts missed corpses. Mouse over a missed one and it comes back.
- Learns which creatures are skinnable as you play.
- Adds the skill a corpse needs, and yours, to its tooltip.
- Works for Herbalism, Mining and Engineering corpses too.
- `/pelt` settings, `/pelt test` preview, `/pelt move` to place the card.
